• Pictures: tap a picture to select it, then drag the corner handles to resize (keeps its shape) or the side handles to stretch it freely
• Drag a selected picture to move it: left or right on the line, or up and down anywhere in the note (it can even go between two lines of text)
• Long-press a picture for its options: full screen (pinch to zoom), get text from the picture, full width, delete
• Extra options depend on the picture: photos can be rotated, retaken or saved to the gallery; gallery pictures can be replaced; scans can be made black & white, rescanned or shared as PDF; drawings can be edited or duplicated
• Sticky notes can use any colour (the full colour picker, with soft paper colours first)
