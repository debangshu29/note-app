• Price memory: on a list, type “milk” and last time's price appears in grey — tap to keep it
• Split with friends: “Rahul paid 1200”, “I paid 800”, “split between me, Rahul, Amit” → a 🤝 chip shows who pays whom
• Lend & borrow: “gave Rahul 500”, “borrowed 200 from mom”, “Rahul ko 500 diye” — tick the line when settled; remind me to ask
• Bills: “Netflix ₹649/month on 5th”, “rent 15000 pm due on 1st” remind you every month and add up to a monthly total
• Scan a bill: ＋ → Scan a bill turns a shop bill into a price list with its total
