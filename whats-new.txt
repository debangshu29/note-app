• Home-screen widgets: quick buttons (note, list, voice, scan), Today (reminders + things to do, tick them right there), a checklist you can tick, and a countdown to a reminder
• Quick capture: long-press the Note icon for New note / New checklist / Voice note / Scan, or add the "New note" tile to your quick settings
• Voice notes: tap 🎤 on the formatting bar and speak, Note types it (and finds reminders in it); pick Hindi or Bengali in Settings → Smart writing
• Read aloud: ⋮ → Read aloud reads the note to you
• Today page: tap "☀ Today" on the home screen for today's reminders, everything still unticked and pinned notes
• Morning brief: one quiet notification each morning with today's plan (Settings → Reminders to change the time or turn it off)
