• Repeating reminders: "gym every Monday at 6 am", "medicine every day at 9 pm", "rent on the 1st of every month", "water every 2 hours", "birthday every year on 12 Nov"
• Labels: write #work or #shopping in a note (or ⋮ → Add a label); chips on the home screen filter your notes
• Recycle bin: deleted notes are kept for 30 days and can be restored (home → ⚙ → Recycle bin)
• Templates: long-press New note for a shopping list, to-do, meeting notes, trip packing, monthly budget or journal
• Grid view and sorting: home → ⚙ shows notes in 2 columns and sorts by edited, created, title or colour
• App lock: PIN + fingerprint for the whole app, and locked notes (⋮ → Lock this note)
• Look & feel: light, dark or pure black (AMOLED), 8 accent colours and 4 text sizes (Settings → Look & feel)
