# Note — feature roadmap

## ✅ Shortlisted (chosen by Debangshu, 11 Nov 2026)

### Everyday use
1. **Home-screen widgets**: quick note, today's reminders, and a pinned checklist you can tick from the home screen
2. **Quick capture**: long-press the app icon for New note / New checklist / Scan / Voice note, plus a quick-settings tile
3. **Voice notes → text**: speak and it types; "remind me to pay rent on 5th" becomes a reminder
4. **Labels**: #work, #shopping… with filter chips on the home screen
5. **Recycle bin**: deleted notes are kept for 30 days
6. **Repeating reminders**: "every Monday at 9", "daily at 8 pm", "on the 1st of every month"
7. **Templates**: shopping list, to-do, meeting notes, trip packing, monthly budget, daily journal
8. **Grid view and sorting**: two columns like Keep; sort by edited, created, title or colour

### Smart features
9. **Monthly spending** from your price lists ("This month ₹8,450 · groceries ₹3,200 · bills ₹2,100")
10. **Scan a bill → instant price list** with the total worked out
11. **Smarter calculator**: "18% GST on 1200", "split 2400 among 3", "EMI 5 lakh 9% 3 years", "5 km in miles", "45 days from today"
12. **Tappable phone numbers and links** with Call / WhatsApp buttons; a "call mom" reminder gets a Call button
13. **Hinglish reminders**: "kal 5 baje mummy ko call", "parso subah 7 baje utha dena"
14. **Offline translate**: English ↔ Hindi / Bengali
15. **Today page**: today's reminders, unticked items from every checklist, pinned notes
16. **Checklist roll-over**: unticked items move to tomorrow
17. **Handwriting → text**
18. **Search inside photos** (text in scans and pictures)
19. **Linked notes**: [[Trip plan]]

## 💡 More ideas (not chosen yet)

### Money
- **Price memory**: "milk ₹60 (last time ₹56)", and it fills in prices you've used before
- **Split with friends**: a trip list works out who owes whom
- **Lend / borrow tracker**: "gave Rahul 500" with a reminder to get it back
- **Bills & subscriptions**: due dates that repeat, plus a monthly total of fixed costs
- **Warranty & expiry tracker**: "warranty till Mar 2027", "milk expires 15 Nov"

### Planning
- **Countdowns**: "exam in 12 days" on the note and in a widget
- **Birthdays & anniversaries**: written once, reminded every year
- **Calendar view**: notes and reminders laid out by date
- **Board view**: a checklist shown as To do / Doing / Done columns
- **Habit tracker**: tick a habit each day and keep a streak
- **Morning brief**: one quiet notification with today's plan
- **Location reminders**: "when I reach home…"

### Writing & study
- **Flashcards / quiz mode** from "Q: … A: …" lines
- **Outline mode**: collapsible headings
- **Read aloud** (on-device text-to-speech)
- **Audio recordings** kept in the note
- **Attach any file** (PDF, document) inside a note
- **Version history**: go back to an earlier version of a note
- **Graph plotting**: "plot y = x^2"

### Privacy & sharing
- **App lock / locked notes** (fingerprint or PIN)
- **Document wallet**: scans of ID cards and certificates in a locked folder
- **Password-protected backups**
- **Share by QR code**: send a note to another phone offline

### Look & feel
- **App accent colour and theme choice** (including pure black AMOLED)
- **Text size setting** for the whole app
